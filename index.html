About

Hi, I'm Colin.

Early adopter and fluent in bespoke AI app development. My professional background is consulting, strategy / finance, and pricing. I build AI tools the way I'd build a model: start with the problem, keep it simple, make it reliable.

I build with AI as a teammate, using a 20-60-20 rule. The first 20% is mine: I decide what I want, write the plan and explain it. The middle 60% is where Claude Code and my other AI tools build and automate the busywork. The last 20% is mine again: I test, refine and iterate until it's exactly right. Because the middle moves so fast, I can lay out several options side by side and pick the best one in minutes.
